Method and coverage
Scores range from -100 (strongly negative) to +100 (strongly positive) across four public developer sources: News & blogs, Dev communities (Reddit, Hacker News, Stack Overflow, GitHub), Review sites (G2, Capterra, Product Hunt), and Social & video (X, Bluesky, LinkedIn, YouTube).
